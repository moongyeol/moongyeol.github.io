Apnotrack

A sleep apnea screening test you can check at home

Breathing during sleep is important information for evaluating sleep apnea.

Apnotrack is a medical device cleared by Korea's Ministry of Food and Drug Safety that records breathing sounds during sleep with a smartphone and uses an AI algorithm to screen for moderate-to-severe sleep apnea.

You can measure your sleep in your usual home environment, without wearing any separate sensor or wearable device.

A medical device for screening moderate-to-severe sleep apnea
(cleared by Korea's Ministry of Food and Drug Safety as a sleep apnea screening device)

When to consider a sleep apnea assessment

  • Severe or persistent snoring
  • Being told that your breathing stops during sleep
  • Choking or gasping for breath during sleep
  • Not feeling refreshed in the morning despite enough sleep
  • Persistent daytime sleepiness or fatigue
  • Recurring morning headaches
  • Wanting to objectively check your breathing during sleep
  • Needing to check your status before and after sleep apnea treatment

What does it evaluate?

Sleep apnea

Analyzes patterns of apnea/hypopnea during sleep and calculates the Apnea-Hypopnea Index (AHI) to screen for moderate-to-severe sleep apnea.

Snoring

Checks the pattern and proportion of snoring occurring during sleep.

Sleep-related indicators

Measured sleep-related indicators such as sleep efficiency and sleep stages can also be reviewed.

Looking at sleep over multiple nights

Sleep status can vary from day to day.

Apnotrack can measure sleep at home over 7 or 30 days, which can be used to look at your usual pattern of sleep apnea and snoring over multiple nights — something a single test can't easily capture.

Apnotrack is a screening test

An accurate diagnosis of sleep apnea requires a separate diagnostic test.

Apnotrack is a medical device for screening moderate-to-severe sleep apnea, and it does not replace the standard sleep test used to confirm a sleep apnea diagnosis. (Polysomnography (PSG) is the representative standard test for diagnosing sleep apnea.)

Apnotrack can therefore be used as “a first-step screening assessment to check whether sleep apnea may be present.”

FAQ

Q. Does Apnotrack diagnose sleep apnea?

Apnotrack is a medical device for screening moderate-to-severe sleep apnea. A final diagnosis isn't made from the test result alone — the medical team evaluates symptoms together with the test result, and arranges a separate detailed sleep test if needed.

Q. Can it replace polysomnography?

No. Apnotrack is a test for screening sleep apnea and does not replace standard sleep tests such as polysomnography.

Q. Can I test with just a smartphone?

Yes. It records your breathing sounds during sleep using your smartphone's microphone, without any separate wearable or sensor.

Q. Is it only a one-day test?

You can measure over 7 or 30 days. The testing period is chosen based on the purpose of the evaluation.

Q. Can it also check snoring?

Yes. It can check the pattern and proportion of snoring that occurs during sleep.

Q. What happens if my AHI is high on the results?

The medical team reviews the results together with your sleep symptoms. If sleep apnea is suspected, they will guide you on any further evaluation and treatment direction needed.
