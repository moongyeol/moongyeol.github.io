Sleep Assessment

Even if you think you're sleeping fine, sleep problems can still be hiding underneath.

Sleep is closely connected not just to how many hours you slept, but to your breathing, sleep continuity, sleep-wake rhythm, and lifestyle pattern.

At MoonGyeol, we check your current sleep status through a basic sleep history and the PSQI (Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index), and combine this with a digital medical device that can measure actual sleep at home when needed.

Based on breath-sound analysis technology, different sleep information is checked depending on the purpose.

  • Apnotrack — sleep apnea screening
  • Chronotrack — sleep-wake rhythm analysis

Sleep Assessment at MoonGyeol

Apnotrack

When sleep-related breathing problems are suspected

Chronotrack

When bedtime/wake time or lifestyle rhythm need to be examined

The assessment needed is selected based on the pattern of your sleep problem and the purpose of evaluation.
