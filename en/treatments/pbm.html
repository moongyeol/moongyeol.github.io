Photobiomodulation

PBM Therapy

Non-invasive photobiomodulation using light

PBM (Photobiomodulation) is a non-invasive method that uses light of specific wavelengths to modulate the biological responses of cells.

Research is ongoing into how near-infrared light affects cellular energy metabolism, mitochondrial function, cerebral blood flow, and nerve cell function.

At Moongyeol, we selectively use PBM based on research into cognitive function and brain health, according to each patient's condition and treatment goals.

When might it be considered?

Cognitive decline

If you feel your memory or information-processing ability has recently declined, or mild cognitive impairment is suspected, your current cognitive status can be evaluated and treatment options reviewed together.

Concentration and brain fog

If reduced concentration or a persistent foggy-headed feeling continues, we look at cognitive, emotional, and sleep status together and, where appropriate, consider it as a supportive treatment method.

Development and cognitive function in children and adolescents

PBM's potential for cognition, behavior, sleep, and brain function is being studied in children with neurodevelopmental characteristics such as autism spectrum disorder.

Age-related cognitive change

If you notice changes in memory or cognitive function with age, your current status can be evaluated and various management approaches reviewed.

When emotional or sleep issues are also present

When cognitive change is accompanied by depressed mood, anxiety, stress, or sleep problems, we evaluate the overall condition and plan any needed treatment together.

※ Whether PBM is applied depends on the patient's condition and treatment goals.

How far has PBM research gone?

A wide range of research on PBM is underway, centered on brain health, cognitive function, and neurological conditions.

Research groups, including medical teams at Harvard Medical School, are studying PBM's effects on the brain and cognitive function, and clinical trials are also underway in people with mild cognitive impairment and early Alzheimer's disease.

Beyond cognitive decline, the potential use of PBM is also being studied in depression and anxiety, sleep problems, neurodevelopmental disorders, and other areas.

Based on the research to date, Moongyeol evaluates each patient's symptoms and condition and, where appropriate, selectively uses PBM alongside other treatments.

Can it be combined with other treatments?

Yes.

Where needed, it can be given alongside existing medication or psychotherapy, and depending on the patient's condition, it may also be combined with non-drug treatments such as Biofeedback or TMS.

PBM can be used as one option among the treatments needed to build a comprehensive plan for each patient.

FAQ

Q. What kind of treatment is PBM?

It is a non-invasive method that uses light of specific wavelengths to modulate the biological responses of cells. Its effects on brain health, cognitive function, and neurological function are being studied.

Q. Is it painful, or does it involve injections?

PBM is performed non-invasively, without cutting the skin or skull or using injections.

Q. Can it help with memory or concentration?

PBM's potential effects on cognitive function and concentration are being studied. Your current cognitive and attention status can be evaluated, and where appropriate it can be considered as a treatment option.

Q. Can it be used for depression or anxiety?

The potential use of PBM for emotional symptoms such as depression and anxiety is also being studied. We comprehensively evaluate your symptoms and treatment history, and where appropriate consider it alongside existing treatment.

Q. How many sessions are needed?

This is planned individually based on treatment goals and current condition. The number and duration of sessions can be adjusted as we check the changes during treatment.
