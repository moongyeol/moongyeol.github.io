Biofeedback Therapy

Understanding your body's signals, and learning to regulate them yourself

Under stress, your heart beats faster, your breathing becomes shallow, and your body tenses up. When this happens repeatedly, tension can become difficult to release, leading to anxiety, sleep difficulties, and fatigue.

Biofeedback is training that lets you check physiological signals such as heart rate and breathing in real time, while learning to regulate your body's tension and arousal through breathing and relaxation.

At MoonGyeol, we use physiological signals including HRV to look at your current state, and provide relaxation and self-regulation training tailored to your individual characteristics.

Who can this help?

Anxiety and tension that won't ease

Frequent worry with ongoing body tension, or physical tension such as a racing heart and uncomfortable breathing

Stress and burnout

Body and mind worn down by ongoing stress, with tension that doesn't release even after resting

Difficulty sleeping

Racing thoughts and body tension at bedtime that make it hard to fall asleep

Panic and physical anxiety

Anxiety accompanied by repeated physical responses such as a racing heart, breathing changes, and tension

※ Biofeedback is not the only way to address these symptoms, and depending on the symptoms and condition, it may be considered together with medication or psychotherapy.

HRV biofeedback has been reported to have significant effects on anxiety and stress symptoms, but methods and study populations vary across research, so it is not a treatment that guarantees the same effect for every patient.

How many sessions are needed?

The number and length of sessions depend on each individual's symptoms and treatment goals. Rather than applying a fixed number of sessions uniformly, we adjust the plan based on the changes and condition observed during treatment.

Can it be combined with other treatments?

Yes. If needed, it can be part of a treatment plan that also includes medication, counseling, TMS, and other treatments.

FAQ

Q. Is Biofeedback a form of medication?

No. It is a non-drug method that trains self-regulation ability by checking your own physiological signals in real time and using breathing and relaxation.

Q. If I have an anxiety disorder, is Biofeedback alone enough?

Not necessarily. Depending on the severity of symptoms and diagnosis, medication or cognitive behavioral therapy may be needed, and Biofeedback is considered together with these when appropriate.

Q. Does it also help with insomnia?

Yes. If stress and tension make it hard to fall asleep, Biofeedback can help you regulate physical tension and arousal. Depending on your sleep condition, it can be combined with other treatments.
